    Whether the Cloud Makes Many Worlds

    The dead gather in a Toronto hall to dispute whether the digital cloud has made of us many worlds. Leibniz, Kant, Socrates and Elijah say it has. McLuhan determines that it has made no world: the table between us is hidden, the bonds beneath it never cut, and the Light that pitched his tent is not in the wind, the earthquake or the fire. With a dance track for Strudel, built from its end.
