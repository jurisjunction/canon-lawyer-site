Essays

Essays in a borrowed voice

Occasional essays on law, theology and the state. Each is written in the register of a writer I admire, and says so. The voice is borrowed; the argument, and its errors, are mine. There are also some biblical reflections and a few colloquies, in the same spirit.

They are filed on a map of the disciplines, sorted by the kind of answer each gives: what is read, computed, found, made, dated, taught, enacted. The map runs from reading and number up to worship, each discipline placed after what it draws on. It is a map, not a course. Follow it wherever an essay leads; the gaps will be filled in when we get there. For a family that wants a course anyway, the Benedict Year walks it in order.

  • Grammar and interpretation · Read

    Reading

    Every text is entered with its readings, and each reading is followed to its end. Interpretation weighs a reading; it never fixes one.

  • Logic and mathematics · Computed

    Number

    What follows from what, and how much. Order comes first, and measure is laid on it, as a date is laid on an event.

    Nothing here yet.

  • The empirical sciences · Found

    Nature

    A finding fixes a fact from its year until a later finding replaces it. Method by division tells us about the parts; it never says what a thing is.

    Draws on Number.

    Nothing here yet.

    Also touching it: The Score

  • The arts, photography among them · Made

    Making

    The sculptor acts on the bronze, and the statue is the bronze given a form, not a new thing. The camera is the same: light acts on the sensor, and the maker gives the picture its form.

    Draws on Nature, Number.

    Nothing here yet.

  • History · Dated

    Memory

    Events stand in an order, and dates are measures laid on it. A claim about who wrote what is checked against that order.

    Draws on Reading, Number.

    • Half of Nothing St Jerome

    Also touching it: The Senate at Capri

  • Philosophy of human nature · Understood

    The Person

    Stone, oak, lion, man, angel: what merely is, what lives, what senses, what understands in a body, what understands without one. One soul is the form of one body, and the will follows the intellect.

    Draws on Nature.

    • The Score Flannery O’Connor
    • The Filler of Voids Simone Weil

    Also touching it: The Meekest Man That Ever Ate in Hall, The Patient Who Cannot Be Guilty

  • Metaphysics · Said by analogy

    Being

    The true, the good and the one are said first of God, and of creatures by participation. We come to know God from his effects, so the road runs from things to him.

    Draws on The Person, Number.

    Nothing here yet.

  • Theology · Taught

    God

    Sources are weighed by their rank, form and date. Nothing crosses the line between Creator and creature unannounced.

    Draws on Being, Memory, Reading.

    • Christ in the Carriage Caryll Houselander

    Also touching it: The Patient Who Cannot Be Guilty, The Filler of Voids, A Plate of Ice

  • Ethics · Judged

    The Act

    What a kind of act is, morally, is taught; the verdict is said of an act, never of a person. What a person knew and willed is claimed, never computed.

    Draws on The Person, Being, God.

    • Of Divers Weights Adam Smith
    • The Meekest Man That Ever Ate in Hall C. S. Lewis
    • The Patient Who Cannot Be Guilty Søren Kierkegaard

    Also touching it: The Rented Hour, Four Dreadful Words

  • Prudence and politics · Supplied

    The City

    The judgment of those responsible for the common good. It is supplied by them, never computed, and the principles it applies come from the act.

    Draws on The Act, Memory.

    • The Senate at Capri Tacitus
    • Four Dreadful Words George Orwell
    • On a People That Made Itself Herbert McCabe

    Also touching it: Of Divers Weights, Half of Nothing

  • Canon and civil law · Enacted

    Law

    Law answers questions of governance and never a question of doctrine. It is ordered to teaching: where it presupposes a teaching, the teaching must hold.

    Draws on The City, The Act, God, Memory, Reading.

    • A Plate of Ice Abraham Joshua Heschel

    Also touching it: Of Divers Weights, On a People That Made Itself

  • Liturgy and the sacraments · Witnessed

    Worship

    The prayer of the Church witnesses and fixes nothing. The rite is matter and form, word, law and time, and the whole person is in it.

    Draws on God, Law, Making.

    • The Rented Hour G. K. Chesterton

    Also touching it: Christ in the Carriage

Not on the map

  • Economics. Taken whole, it lays market findings on moral questions and says what a man is from a model reached by division. Taken apart, its findings belong to Nature, justice in exchange to The Act, and policy to The City.
  • Psychology. The same two faults: guilt treated as nothing but a symptom, and a moral question rested on a finding. Its findings belong to Nature, the soul to The Person, and guilt to The Act.
  • Folded in. Some disciplines have no kind of answer of their own and live inside others: computer science in Number and Making; medicine in Nature and Making, ordered by The Act; music and photography in Making, with Number and Nature; rhetoric in Making and Reading, since the speech is a thing made and the persuasion of another’s mind is never computed.
