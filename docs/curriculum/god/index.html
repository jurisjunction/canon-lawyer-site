God

“Sources are weighed by their rank, form and date. Nothing crosses the line between Creator and creature unannounced.”

The kind of answer: taught · God on the map

In a child’s words. When sources disagree, ask who said it, in what kind of act, and when. And never mix up the Creator and a creature without saying so.

The Institute habit

At the Institute every word from above counted as truth, whoever said it, in whatever form, on whatever date. S.Q. Pedalian trusted Mr. Curtain over his own conscience.

The book

The prequel’s end: a stranger’s kindness on a train, and Nicholas turning back to help the other orphans instead of only escaping.

Week 1 · the kind of answer

Rank, form and date. Take six sources (a verse of Scripture, a creed, an encyclical, the Catechism, a theologian’s book, an interview) and give each its rank, form and date. Which can settle a question, and which can only weigh on it?

Week 2 · the essay

Christ in the Carriage, in the register of Caryll Houselander, read whole. Ask: who would be the hardest person for you to see that way?

Week 3 · the case

The Immaculate Conception. In 1800 the question is open: St Bernard, St Bonaventure and St Thomas Aquinas had all held otherwise. Pius IX defined it in 1854, and by 1860 it is settled; the three Doctors stand noted as having held against what was later defined, and are not condemned for it. A saint can be wrong on an open question, and an act of the right rank and form can close it. Set it beside Unit 3: the censure of 1616 failed because it laid heresy on a fact; this definition stands because it is the right kind of act on the right kind of question.
