Michaelmas · Weeks 4–7

Creation

The Vigil opens with creation because that night is a new creation.

The text at the centre: Genesis 1:1–2:2

A reading of the Easter Vigil

Why it is here

John dates the resurrection to the first day of the week, and he is not reporting a calendar. The flood is a branch, one of the older Vigil prophecies: it sets up the sea three units early, so that when Israel goes down into the water he has already seen water that drowns and rescues in one motion. 1 Peter 3:20–21 calls it baptism outright.

What is it for?

The rainbow. The text answers: a sign, so that God will remember the covenant (Genesis 9:15–16).

Unanswerable

What’s the seventh day for, if the work was finished on the sixth?
