Hilary · Weeks 15–18

The Sea

The largest unit and the heart of the year.

The text at the centre: Exodus 12; Exodus 14:15–15:1

A reading of the Easter Vigil

Why it is here

Exodus 12 is an older Vigil prophecy restored here, and Holy Thursday’s first reading. Melito’s Easter homily is the centrepiece of the whole year: second-century, hammering and repetitive, built to be heard. Read it aloud; the child will feel it where they don’t follow it. Jonah comes last because Christ gives it as his own sign, and a child who has watched Israel come up out of the sea should meet at once a man who goes down into it and comes back on the third day.

What is it for?

The blood on the doorposts. The text answers: a sign, so that the destroyer passes over (Exodus 12:13).
