Essays in a borrowed voice

Occasional essays on law, theology and the state. Each is written in the register of a writer I admire, and says so. The voice is borrowed; the argument, and its errors, are mine. There are also some biblical reflections, in the same spirit.

  • In the register of G. K. Chesterton · October 2026

    The Rented Hour

    There is a small glowing oblong in every pocket, and inside it is a grid of twenty-minute rectangles, given away to strangers. What the digital calendar has done to the season, the promise and the feast, and to the liberty, friendship and fallow ground of the man who owns it; and the one white rectangle worth defending.
