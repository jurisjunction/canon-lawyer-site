My approach

Faithful to the law, and to you

Honest assessments

I will tell you plainly what the law says, how strong your position looks, and what it will realistically take. No one can promise an outcome from a Church tribunal, and I won’t.

Respect for the Church

Advocacy in the Church serves justice and the truth. I work respectfully with tribunals and authorities while insisting that your rights are fully honoured.

Deadlines in hand

Canonical time limits are short and often “peremptory”: once they pass, they cannot be extended. I track them so you don’t have to.

Plain language

Latin terms and canon numbers are my job, not yours. You’ll always understand where things stand and why.
